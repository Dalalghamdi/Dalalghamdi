## 📚 Research

My research interests center on the use of **ontologies and semantic technologies in biomedical informatics**, particularly for representing genomic and biobanking data and enabling computational knowledge discovery.

A major focus of my work has been the development of the **Next Generation Biobanking Ontology (NGBO)**, designed to extend semantic representation of biobanking information to include omics-related contextual data.
